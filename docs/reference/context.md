## 1. Coverage (FWD / EHRI)

Source: https://data.opm.gov/resources/data-sources (accessed 2026-11-17)

- Executive branch: EHRI "covers all federal civilian employees of the Executive branch, except" the following: U.S. Postal Service, Postal Rate Commission, CIA, NSA, DIA, NGA, ODNI, White House Office, Office and Official Residence of the Vice President, Census Bureau temporary workers, State Department Foreign Service personnel, TVA, Federal Reserve Board of Governors (CFPB is included as a subelement of the Federal Reserve), PHS Commissioned Corps, non-appropriated fund employees, foreign nationals overseas, Executive Residence, and the President's Intelligence Advisory Board.
- Legislative branch: "EHRI generally does not include employees of the Legislative branch." Included: GPO, Eisenhower Memorial Commission, Financial Crisis Inquiry Commission, Reagan Centennial Commission, MedPAC, U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission, and USCIRF.
- Judicial branch: generally excluded. Only the U.S. Tax Court is included.
- Status (the employment snapshot): "all active employees (in either a pay or non-pay status)" as of the last day of each month.
- Accessions and separations come from the Dynamics dataset. Source: https://data.opm.gov/info-and-help/getting-started (accessed 2026-11-17). Accessions "Includes new hires, transfers, and rehires." Separations "Includes retirements, resignations, terminations, and deaths." Federal employees: "Excludes military personnel."
- The same page says FWD omits FedScope's disability, gender, race/national origin and ethnicity fields, citing EOs 14151, 14168 and 14173.

## 3. Deferred Resignation Program (DRP)

- Offer date and separation date: the OPM memo of 2025-01-28 sets resignation "effective on September 30, 2025". https://www.opm.gov/media/3oaf3vs0/opm-guidance-memo-re-deferred-resignation-program-01-28-25-final.pdf
- Original deadline: the same memo names **6 February 2025** ("missed the February 6, 2025 deadline"). The program actually closed at 7:20 pm ET on **12 February 2025**: https://www.opm.gov/about-us/fork/
- Retirement-eligible: "If your retirement date is between October 1, 2025 and December 31, 2025", the deferred resignation date "will be extended to match your retirement date." https://www.opm.gov/about-us/fork/faq/ . FWD states the same rule: separate "by September 30, 2025 (or up to December 31, 2025 if retirement eligible)". https://data.opm.gov/explore-data/analytics/workforce-changes
- Eligibility: "all full-time federal employees except" military, USPS, immigration enforcement, national security, public safety, and positions excluded by the agency (fork FAQ).
- FY2026 agency-specific DRP. OPM bulletin "2026 Agency-Specific Deferred Resignation Program and Voluntary Early Retirement Authority Guidance", https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USOPM/bulletins/3ff4a7e :
  - "during FY 2026, agencies may use Deferred Resignation Program (DRP) Agreements of up to six months", subject to budget and appropriations.
  - OPM will approve VERA requests "through the end of CY 2026".
  - Agreements "of up to 12 weeks may also be offered" in connection with performance or conduct issues.
  - Agencies report DRP use to tracking@opm.gov monthly.
  - The bulletin date (11 Dec 2025 per the brief) was **not shown** on the page as read.
- Official count: FWD Workforce Changes shows "141,084 Federal employees took the Deferred Resignation Program (DRP)". This counts processed DRP separation actions, not acceptances. FWD adds: "Additional DRP actions are expected to be processed over time". https://data.opm.gov/explore-data/analytics/workforce-changes

## 5. Probationary terminations and codes

- The FWD Data Dictionary (https://data.opm.gov/FWD%20Data%20Dictionary.xlsx, linked from the API page) lists every field in the Employment, Accessions and Separations files. No field identifies probationary or trial-period status, the nature-of-action code, or the legal authority. The Separations file carries `separation_category` / `separation_category_code`, defined as "The code representing the personnel action that resulted in the separation", plus `drp_indicator`, `tenure` and `appointment_type`.
- **Finding:** the public FWD files cannot isolate probationary terminations. This is our inference from the full field list, not an OPM statement.
- Code definitions. The dictionary gives field-level definitions only, with one sample code per field: `SC` = "QUIT" (separations) and `AC` = "NEW HIRE - COMPETITIVE SERVICE APPOINTMENT" (accessions). No official OPM page opened this session defines the individual codes SA, SB, SD, SE, SG, SH, SJ, SL, AA, AB, AD or AE. See "Not verified".
