# Pre-publication verification (2026-11-16)

Checked before any public deployment, as the owner required. Each line cites the source read on this date.

## OPM data release terms

| Question | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Is there a licence or attribution requirement for FWD files? | None found. data.opm.gov links only a Data Release Policy and a privacy notice; the policy governs what OPM withholds, not how released data may be reused. Works of the U.S. government are not subject to copyright in the U.S. (17 U.S.C. § 105). | data.opm.gov/info-and-help/getting-started; OPM Data Release Policy (December 2024), opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/data-analysis-documentation/data-policy-guidance/data-standards/data-release-policy-december-2024.pdf |
| Why is pay hidden for about half of records? | The policy redacts adjusted basic pay and locality pay area (and duty-station detail) for security and sensitive agencies (FBI, DEA, ATF, TTB, USSS, CBP, ICE, BOP and U.S. Attorneys' Offices among them) and for a list of sensitive occupational series. This matches the measured pattern (hidden pay concentrated in Defense components, DHS and DOJ; locality hidden with pay). | Same policy |
| What else is never released? | Name-linked sensitive fields such as race, ethnicity, sex, disability, performance rating, and adverse-action codes. This confirms limitation L5 (no protected-class or performance analysis). | Same policy |

**Practice adopted:** cite OPM as the source on every page, state that Workforce Atlas is independent and not affiliated with OPM, and publish only aggregates (the site never shows a record).

## Deferred Resignation Program (DRP)

| Fact | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Name | "Deferred Resignation Program", offered government-wide by OPM | OPM guidance memo, 28 January 2025 (opm.gov/media/cbklseim/opm-guidance-memo-re-deferred-resignation-program-1-28-2025.pdf) |
| Offer date | Emailed to employees on 28 January 2025 | opm.gov/about-us/fork/original-email-to-employees |
| Closing | Closed 12 February 2025, 7:20 pm ET | opm.gov/about-us/fork |
| Separation date | By 30 September 2025, with pay and benefits on administrative leave until then; up to 31 December 2025 if retirement-eligible | OPM legality memo, 4 February 2025; data.opm.gov/explore-data/analytics/workforce-changes |
| Exclusions | Military personnel, Postal Service employees, positions in immigration enforcement, national security and public safety, and positions excluded by the employing agency | opm.gov/about-us/fork/faq |

**Updated 2026-11-17:** the earlier "not verified" note on follow-on rounds is superseded. OPM's bulletin "2026 Agency-Specific DRP and VERA Guidance" (content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USOPM/bulletins/3ff4a7e) lets agencies use their own deferred resignation agreements of up to six months in FY2026. The original deadline was 6 February 2025; the program closed on 12 February 2025. Full verified context, policy timeline and coverage: `docs/reference/context.md`.
